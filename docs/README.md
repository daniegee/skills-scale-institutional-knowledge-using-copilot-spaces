# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides quick access to OctoAcme's standardized project management practices and serves as the entry point for all process documents in this folder. Whether you are onboarding to the team or looking to understand how OctoAcme delivers projects, this guide will orient you to the key workflows, roles, and artifacts that keep our teams aligned and accountable.

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle that begins with **initiation**—where a problem statement, stakeholders, and high-level timeline are established—and moves through **planning**, where scope, milestones, resources, and dependencies are defined. During **execution and tracking**, the delivery team builds, tests, and iterates in short cycles, maintaining transparency through standups, sprint backlogs, and regular status reports to stakeholders.

Throughout the project, **risk and communication** practices ensure that issues are surfaced early, owners are assigned, and the right people stay informed on a consistent cadence (weekly PM/PdM syncs, twice-weekly standups, and monthly stakeholder updates). When a feature or product increment is ready, the **release and deployment** process governs testing, approvals, and go-live activities—ensuring quality and coordinated rollout. After each significant delivery, the team conducts **retrospectives** to capture learnings, identify process improvements, and embed those insights into future iterations.

Underpinning all of these processes are clearly defined **roles and personas**: Project Managers coordinate delivery and manage risk; Product Managers own the vision and backlog; Developers implement and test; and Stakeholders provide input and approvals. This Copilot Space centralizes these docs so they are searchable, versioned, and easy to reference at any point in the project lifecycle.
